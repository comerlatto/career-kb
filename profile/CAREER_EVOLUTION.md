# Career Evolution

## Purpose

Document the progression of my professional career, highlighting how my responsibilities, technical skills and business perspective evolved over time.

This document is intended to provide context for resumes, interviews and career-related content generation.

---

## Evolution

### Commercial Operations (2012–2015)

Started supporting the commercial area through reports, sales indicators and process automation.

Core skills developed:

- Reporting
- KPIs
- Process automation
- Business support

---

### Commercial Analytics & Leadership (2015–2019)

Expanded responsibilities to commercial analysis, team leadership and business process management.

Core skills developed:

- Team leadership
- Business Intelligence
- SQL
- Performance analysis
- Process improvement

---

### Business Intelligence (2019–2022)

Transitioned to BI-focused roles, supporting Planning and Procurement through dashboards, forecasting, inventory analysis and decision support.

Core skills developed:

- Power BI
- Forecast
- Supply Chain
- Inventory analytics
- MySQL
- Business metrics

---

### Analytics Engineering (2022–Present)

Transitioned to Analytics Engineering, participating in the modernization of the company's analytical platform.

Core skills developed:

- PostgreSQL
- dbt
- Airbyte
- Data Warehouse
- ELT
- Semantic models
- Data governance
- Generative AI

---

## Career Pattern

The evolution of my career follows a consistent path:

Commercial Operations
→ Business Analytics
→ Business Intelligence
→ Analytics Engineering

Throughout this journey, the common element has always been the use of data to support better business decisions.
